## **Aim:**

To test and compare how different prompting patterns respond to naïve prompts (broad, unstructured) versus basic structured prompts (clear, detailed, refined) across multiple scenarios.
The purpose is to analyze the **quality**, **accuracy**, and **depth** of AI-generated responses when prompt clarity is varied.

---

## **AI Tools Required:**

• ChatGPT
• Google Gemini
• Internet-connected device

---

## **Explanation:**

### **Define the Two Prompt Types**

#### **1. Naïve Prompt**

A vague, short, open-ended instruction that lacks context.
Example: *“Explain robotics.”*

#### **2. Basic Structured Prompt**

A clear and detailed prompt that specifies context, structure, and required output.
Example:
*“Explain industrial robotics in 120 words, including definition, components, two applications, and one real-time example.”*

Using these two prompt patterns, multiple scenarios were tested, and the responses were recorded for comparison.

---

# **Procedure**

---

## **Step 1: Prepare Multiple Test Scenarios**

The following scenarios were selected as required:

1. Creative story generation
2. Factual question answering
3. Summarization
4. Advice / recommendations

For each scenario, a **naïve** and a **basic structured** prompt were prepared.

---

## **Step 2: Run Experiments with ChatGPT and Gemini**

Both AI tools were given the **same naïve** and **same structured** prompts.
The responses were recorded and compared based on **quality**, **accuracy**, and **depth**.

# **Analysis and Discussion**

### **Quality:**

Structured prompts produced richer, clearer, and more descriptive responses.

### **Accuracy:**

Basic prompts ensured factual correctness by providing boundaries and context.

### **Depth:**

Naïve prompts resulted in surface-level information, whereas structured prompts generated detailed and insightful content.

### **Observation:**

Naïve prompts only matched structured prompts in extremely simple tasks (e.g., single-sentence facts).
For all other cases, structured prompts were significantly better.

---

# **Conclusion**

This study confirms that **prompt clarity directly influences the quality, accuracy, and depth of AI outputs**.
Basic structured prompts consistently generated superior results across all scenarios.
Therefore, structured prompt engineering is essential for professional, academic, and technical applications.
